ⓘ About the sim

What you see is the simulator's real run, played back: every car, machine and line of lots is where the simulator had it. One floor tile is 3 m. Prices are made up for the game. The small fab is our own design, and the full fab is built from the SMT2020 testbed. Neither is a real fab.

The process

This fab makes one product. A lot of 25 wafers enters every 30 minutes and visits these 18 steps in order, each on any free machine of its kind. A real fab's product takes hundreds of steps: 583 for one of the SMT2020 testbed's two products. This one is kept small so you can see every step.
